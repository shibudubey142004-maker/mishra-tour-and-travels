Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you book.
How do I know how many seats are left?

Select a travel date and passenger count. The website checks the live availability for that date.

Will I receive a booking code?

Yes. A unique booking code is generated when your booking is successfully created.

How can I get help?

Use the WhatsApp button to contact Mishra Tour and Travels with your booking details.

Can I pay online right now?

The current site uses a clearly marked test UPI flow. Real payment gateway processing will be added before launch.

What happens after I cancel?

When an eligible booking is cancelled by the admin, its reserved seats are restored to availability.

Is my booking stored securely?

Bookings are stored in Supabase and managed through the site's protected booking workflow.
